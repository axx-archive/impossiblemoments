NO. 002The Format

An arena event. Not a film.

No one in the building knows how it ends.

Impossible Fights is a new live-event format. Two fighters, restored to their primes, meet in front of a full arena — the walkouts, the corners, the announcer, the bell. The crowd is real. The night is real. The fight is impossible.

Dozens of endings are filmed for every bout. The knockout in the second. The war that goes the distance. The comeback off the canvas. On fight night one of them happens, and nobody in the building knows which — not the crowd, not the crew, not the arena staff. A film has an ending. A fight has a result.

NO. 002The Thesis

The greatest fights in history never happened.

Every bar, every gym, every comment section. The same arguments, never settled.

Who wins is the oldest question in sports. Across eras, across weight classes, across disciplines. The arguments were unanswerable for one reason: time lets each fighter exist once. One era. One body. One sport. Then the window closes.

Likeness rights collapse that constraint. Every fighter, in their prime, available to be matched. The argument stops being an argument. It becomes a booking.
